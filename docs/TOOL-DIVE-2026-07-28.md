## Doctrine adjudication (ACE-FCA / WSFF / 12-factor)

ACE-FCA's "review the plan, not the diff" assumes a deeply engaged interactive operator —
disqualified in its mid-flight form. Three translated forms fit batched attention: (1) plan
artifact mandatory + archived on the PR for every non-oneshot flight; (2) plans-as-alignment
digest in the morning report (honest: alignment, not a slop gate); (3) plan-approval label tier
for effort:max only, parked at the P2 split boundary where nothing is running — rides the same
morning batch as issue approvals (label must be registered in labels.py + adopt run, per i165).
Model plan cross-review with park authority covers the middle tier (plan review is mostly
pin-checking facts, which cross-family models do well).

WSFF's "lights-off factories degrade at 3–6 months" is the honest open attack on the goal: our
~1 month of history cannot refute it; our bad merges so far are identity/process holes, not
measured slop collapse. But superlooper is not lights-off in Dex's sense (human-authored issues,
cross-family review, e2e proof, owner-directed architecture). Response: instrument, don't argue —
a slop/complexity trend line in the morning report, sustained degradation = systemic stop.
Revisit at month three with data.

Ralph-loop aside (ace-fca): "not entirely sure that it is dumb" — superlooper is Ralph with
discipline; the doctrine read confirms the architecture rather than challenging it.
